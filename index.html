the ritual

The recipe is only half the story.

Comfort lives in the sequence around the plate: smell, sound, waiting, serving and staying.

01

Hear the kitchen.

Tempering crackles. A lid lifts. Someone calls you to the table.

02

Take the first bite.

Steam arrives first, then the familiar taste that needs no explanation.

03

Serve generously.

A little extra is often the unofficial measurement of care.

04

Stay a while.

The best part of dinner can be the conversation after the plate is empty.
