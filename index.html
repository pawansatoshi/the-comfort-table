the ritual

The recipe is only half the story.

Comfort food lives in the sequence around the plate: smell, sound, waiting, serving, sharing.

01

Hear the kitchen.

Every home has a sound that means food is almost ready.

02

Lift the lid.

Steam arrives first. Everyone suddenly remembers they are hungry.

03

Serve generously.

A little extra is the unofficial measurement of love.

04

Stay a while.

The best part of dinner is sometimes the conversation after.
